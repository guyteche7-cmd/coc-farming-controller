# Research Notes — 2026-08-22

This document is included as a supporting artifact for the six core project documents.

## Current findings

### PC availability

Clash of Clans is officially available on Windows through Google Play Games. Supercell states that the PC release supports mouse controls, optimized graphics, and gameplay equivalent to the mobile version. [Supercell, 2023](https://supercell.com/en/news/clash-games-live-on-pc/) [Supercell Support](https://support.supercell.com/clash-royale/en/articles/clash-of-clans-clash-royale-now-on-pc-with-google-play-games.html)

### Heroes

The current official support page lists six Home Village Heroes, including Dragon Duke, with up to four attacking Hero slots. Heroes have battle abilities and Hero Equipment. [Supercell Support: About Heroes & Pets](https://support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/about-heroes-pets-9.html)

### Dragon Duke

Supercell introduced Dragon Duke for Town Hall 15+ as a new Hero with unique mechanics in the February 2026 update. [Supercell: February 2026 update](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/release-notes/the-february-update-has-escaped/)

### Totem Spell

Totem is a real Clash of Clans spell. Town Hall 18-era release notes describe it as a spell that distracts defenses and soaks damage. In the February 2026 update, its radius changed from 6 tiles to 5 and its damage changed to 0. [Supercell: Town Hall 18](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/release-notes/town-hall-18-crash-lands-update/) [Supercell: February 2026 update](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/release-notes/the-february-update-has-escaped/)

### Walls

Supercell's recent updates describe multiple-wall selection for same-level walls, with the ability to add 1 or 10 at a time and continue until the upgrade cost exceeds resource capacity or no eligible walls remain. [Supercell](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/news/get-geared-up-for-the-latest-update--3/)

### Fair Play / automation risk

Supercell's current Fair Play guidance explicitly classifies bots, mods, third-party software, and gameplay automation scripts as unacceptable behavior/cheating and notes that penalties may apply. This is a project risk, not something the software should attempt to bypass. [Supercell Support: Don't Be a Cheat](https://ingame.support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/fair-play-dont-be-a-cheat.html?q=1779807778) [Supercell Support: List of Unacceptable Behavior](https://support.supercell.com/clash-of-clans/en/articles/list-of-unacceptable-behavior.html)
